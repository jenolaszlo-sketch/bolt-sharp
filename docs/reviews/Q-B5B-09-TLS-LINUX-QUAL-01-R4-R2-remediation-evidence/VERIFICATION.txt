1. DEFECT
---------
Verifier.load_peer_receipt returned only the FIRST record with record_type
"peer-receipt" and ignored all others, so duplicate, contradictory, or unrelated
appended receipt records silently passed verification. Three cases were demonstrated
by the independent reviewer to produce VERIFIER: PASS on contradictory evidence.

2. REMEDIATION
--------------
load_peer_receipt -> load_peer_receipts (returns EVERY peer-receipt record).
verify_cell_l7 now requires EXACTLY ONE per qualifying L7 attempt:
  zero  -> "peer receipt journal record missing"
  > 1   -> "exactly one peer-receipt record is required, found N"
           plus a specific error for every extra record bound to another attempt/cell.

The record_type filter keeps the rule narrow: the real peer's peer-start / tls /
peer-eof records carry no record_type and remain valid.

Preserved L7 contracts (unchanged): SHA-256 equality between journal expectation and
peer receipt; exact message-byte-count equality; attempt and cell binding; valid Bolt
message framing; receipt self-consistency.

Files changed: verify_r1.py, verifier_selftest.py. No harness, production, peer, or
frozen-test change.
