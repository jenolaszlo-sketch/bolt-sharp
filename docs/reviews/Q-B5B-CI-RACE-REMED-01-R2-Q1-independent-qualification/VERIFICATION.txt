10. F-02 EVIDENCE (unbalanced accept accounting)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Code: src/BoltSharp/Transport/BoltTcpServer.cs:456-461 (general catch reports
ConnectionFailed with id "listener"); BoltTransportDiagnostics.cs:111-113
unconditionally decrements ConnectionsActive on ConnectionFailed.

(BoltTransportDiagnostics.cs:111-113) and the same general-catch report exists

(reports only after ConnectionAccepted).
