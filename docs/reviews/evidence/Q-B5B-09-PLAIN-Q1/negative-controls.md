# Integrated negative controls

All trials used Windows Debug, the pinned production entry point and candidate tests in an isolated copy at `20fbc461c0257620db28fb78a463218b242455bc`. Run `dotnet test tests/BoltSharp.Tests/BoltSharp.Tests.csproj --no-restore -nodeReuse:false --filter <case> --logger 'trx;LogFileName=nc-NN.trx'`. The runner, exact patches, logs and TRX results are in the evidence archive. The runner restores every edited path from its original bytes and checks the detached revision and clean status after each case. The 12-case unmodified control passed before and after the fault series.

| Control | Mutation and requirement | Reached path, detecting oracle and outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NC-01 | R04. Re-enable Ready after marking the refusal. In the terminal write delegate read a queued frame with the retired reader and pass it to the session, discarding its result. | Exact terminal FAILURE was read. Small and delayed tails opened a provider preparation once with no additional reply. Two of five failing rows specifically hit the zero-provider assertion. Other rows reflect discarded-byte or message-count consequences and were unnecessary for detection. |
| NC-02 | R09/R10. Suppress the helper's actual socket close and the enclosing connection's client disposal. | An admitted drain entered and was stopped under its expected listener outcome. The captured handle remained open and valid after StopAsync. The failed assertion tested actual handle closure. |
| NC-03 | R06. Remove listener-token registration in the terminal owner. | Stop ran while an admitted drain awaited real peer input. It ended on the independent drain deadline. The failed oracle observed drain-deadline where listener-shutdown was required. |
| NC-04 | R05. After the 205 ms write-start hold, reset the total timer at drain entry and use a fresh drain elapsed origin. The drain still has its own 75 ms limit. | The exact terminal FAILURE was read. The stalled drain ran to its own timer. The original deadline oracle expected total-deadline and observed drain-deadline, which can win only after the drain's full sub-budget in this mutation. |
| NC-05 | R08. Increase the 8 KiB byte cap to 12 KiB. | The large tail reached the discard loop and exhausted 12,288 bytes. The 8,192-byte oracle failed; the five other cases passed. |
| NC-06 | R09. Allow two permits per listener, still clamped to MaxConnections minus one. | At connection limit 4 the three contenders received two permits. The cap assertion expected one. The single- and zero-permit cases passed. |
| NC-07 | R12. Pass the helper into an authenticated TLS connection with a zero-permit policy. Use the owning NetworkStream as the helper argument while preserving encrypted writing through the delegate. | TLS successfully read the exact terminal FAILURE over SslStream. Correlated write-start, write-complete and disposed/capacity events proved forbidden helper entry. Zero permits prevented raw reads and excluded a cast or fixture failure. |

Seven intended detections. The unrelated NC-01 rows are preserved in the raw outputs. F01 was found in a separate unmutated-lifecycle probe and is outside this control count.
